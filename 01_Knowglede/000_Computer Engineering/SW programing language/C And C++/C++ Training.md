# C++ Training

I have yet to hear about a good C++ training :D

What I would recommend to beginners is checking out the "**Back to Basics" tracks of CppCon** [https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cppcon+back+to+basics](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cppcon+back+to+basics)  
These talks provide nice overviews over core C++ concepts, typically with nice examples, DOs and DONTs.

Then, I think it's best to "just do it" and start coding.  
The problem is that it's often tricky to find something motivating to work on in a language one is not too proficient at.  
Here I typically recommend websites that provide fun challenges:

- **CodinGame** is a platform that has a ton of programming puzzles in a gamified way.  
    Write the code directly in your browser, no IDE required, >30 languages to choose from.  
    After solving, you can see the solutions of others to learn from their approaches.  
    Also offers competitive coding competitions if that's your cup of tea.  
    [https://www.codingame.com/home](https://www.codingame.com/home)
- **Project Euler** is a more "neutral" approach to the same problem:  
    Mathematical puzzles to solve, all you upload is the final result so you can solve them in whatever language you like.  
    Definitely the hardest puzzles of this list, as you really need a lot of maths for the later ones.  
    [https://projecteuler.net/](https://projecteuler.net/)
- Last but not least: **Advent of Code**  
    The, in my opinion, highest quality and most motivating collection of challenges.  
    Basically an advent calendar every year with a cool storyline and challenges of increasing difficulty.  
    The calendars of past years are all available as well.  
    Like with Project Euler, all you upload is the final result so you can solve them in whatever language you like.  
    A dedicated subreddit has users compare their solutions in all kinds of languages.  
    [https://adventofcode.com/2025/about](https://adventofcode.com/2025/about)
