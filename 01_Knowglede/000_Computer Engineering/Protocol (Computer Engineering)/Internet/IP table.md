[Firewalls](https://www.digitalocean.com/community/tutorials/what-is-a-firewall-and-how-does-it-work) are an important tool that can be configured to protect your servers and infrastructure. In the Linux ecosystem, `iptables` is a widely used firewall tool that works with the kernel’s `netfilter` packet filtering framework. Creating reliable firewall policies can be daunting, due to complex syntax and the number of interrelated parts involved.
