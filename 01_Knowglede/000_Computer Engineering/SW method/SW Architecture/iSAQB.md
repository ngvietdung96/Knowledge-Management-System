# iSAQB

![[RecommendReadingBookiSAQB.png]]

### What does iSAQB mean for my career?
The non-profit organization "International Software Architecture Qualification Board", iSAQB for short, focuses on the promotion of software architects and developers. The main goal of this organization is to guarantee the professional quality of the training and examination for software architects. Consequently, the main task of iSAQB is to set the rules for certification exams and to issue licenses to training providers. The association consists of active members who contribute to the development of syllabuses and the development of exam questions and the glossary. [See all details here.](https://www.isaqb.org/)
### iSAQB certification is categorized into three levels:
1. Foundation Level (CPSA-F) - conveys the basics of software architecture.
2. Advanced Level (CPSA-A) - is proof of knowledge of advanced software architecture.
3. Expert Level - is currently still being planned.

### What is the iSAQB certification for?
The role of a software architect is becoming increasingly important in the software development industry and for this reason requires extensive training. The iSAQB Foundation Level exam is the most basic of the three iSAQB levels and is intended for software architects, developers, and any IT staff working on software projects.

❖ CPSA Foundation Level
The CPSA F certification is the first level of the CPSA series. This certification is based on a framework of core competencies and training modules. Each module has its own requirements, but the training must have a certain minimum number of credits in order to qualify for the exam. In addition, the curriculum covers a range of tools and techniques ranging from modeling to architecture. It also focuses on domain knowledge and tactical design. [Learn more about the foundation-level course here.](https://tecnovy.com/en/isaqb/foundation-cpsa-f)

❖ CPSA Advanced Level
iSAQB offers two advanced certification levels for software architects, one of which is CPSA-A or Certified Professional in Software Architecture. The Advanced Level certification requires 70 credits, with 10 credits for each skill area. Existing certifications can also be applied for to increase your points. ISAQB CPSA certificates are valid for life. [See the available advanced-level courses here.](https://tecnovy.com/en/isaqb)

❖ Expert level
The advanced Expert Level focuses on three distinct areas of expertise: solving complex engineering problems, ensuring architectural design, and teaching architecture.
