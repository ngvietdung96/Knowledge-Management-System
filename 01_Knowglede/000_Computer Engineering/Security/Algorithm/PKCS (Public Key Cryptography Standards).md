# Overview:
**Public Key Cryptography Standards** (**PKCS**) are a group of [public-key cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public-key_cryptography "Public-key cryptography") standards devised and published by [RSA Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_Security "RSA Security") LLC, starting in the early 1990s. The company published the standards to promote the use of the cryptography techniques for which they had [patents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent "Patent"), such as the [RSA algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_algorithm "RSA algorithm"), the [Schnorr signature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schnorr_signature "Schnorr signature") algorithm and several others. 
Though not [industry standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_computer_standards "List of computer standards") (because the company retained control over them), some of the standards have begun to move into the "[standards track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standards_track "Standards track")" processes of relevant [standards organizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standards_organization "Standards organization") in recent years, such as the [IETF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IETF "IETF") and the [PKIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKIX "PKIX") working group.

**Key Updates (2023–2024):**

- Integration of PKCS #7 and PKCS #12 into broader standards like S/MIME and TLS.
- Evolution of PKCS #11 to support newer hardware and cloud services.
- Involvement of PKCS standards in post-quantum cryptography efforts, with NIST's ongoing standardization.
- Growing adoption of PKCS standards in the context of blockchain and digital assets.



[What are Public Key Cryptography Standards (PKCS)? Meaning, Specifications, and Importance](https://www.spiceworks.com/it-security/network-security/articles/what-is-pkcs/#:~:text=Public%20key%20cryptography%20standards%20are,key%20infrastructure%20in%20data%20exchange.&text=Public%20key%20cryptography%20standards%20(PKCS)%20are%20defined%20as%20a%20set,key%20infrastructure%20to%20exchange%20information)
PKCS provide a framework and structure for all communication via the [[Public Key Infrastructure  (PKI)|Public key Infrastructure (PKI)]].They give abstract syntax and algorithm details that can be adapted across different technologies.
