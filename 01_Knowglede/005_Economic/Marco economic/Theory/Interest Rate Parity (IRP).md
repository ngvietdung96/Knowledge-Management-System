The interest rate parity (IRP) is a theory regarding the relationship between the [spot exchange rate](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/career-map/sell-side/capital-markets/spot-price/) and the expected spot rate or [forward](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/derivatives/futures-forwards/) exchange rate of two currencies, based on [interest rates](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/commercial-lending/interest-rate/). The theory holds that the forward exchange rate should be equal to the spot currency exchange rate _times_ the interest rate of the home country, divided by the interest rate of the foreign country.
